Iteration 2 — 2026-08-16 — Issue 02: Pick a Style, see the weights  [DONE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

What I did
- Built the domain module in src/lib/dough/: styles.ts (five Presets), solve.ts
  (the Anchor arithmetic and rounding), index.ts (the public barrel), and
  solve.test.ts. Added `percentages` to SolvedRecipe and `freshYeastPercent` to
  Style, both required by the ticket.
- Built the two screens: StylePicker.svelte lists the five Styles with names,
  blurbs and default Batch; Workbench.svelte holds the solved weights in a
  sticky header with Baker's Percentages, plus Batch steppers and the Style's
  bake string. +page.svelte switches between them.
- Wrote src/lib/format.ts for gram and percentage display.
- 15 tests, written red-green one slice at a time against solve() — the seam the
  PRD pre-agreed. Coverage: the Anchor, Hydration trading flour for water, the
  summing rule across all five Styles, four awkward Batch sizes, and the
  per-ingredient rounding units.

Key decisions and reasoning
- FLOUR CARRIES A TENTH OF A GRAM. Two acceptance criteria contradict each
  other: flour cannot be whole and also absorb the rounding drift, because the
  tenths the small ingredients round away have to land somewhere. Neapolitan:
  water 375 + salt 16.9 + yeast 4.5 leaves flour at 603.6; rounding it to 604
  makes four 250 g balls read 1000.4 g. Kept exact summing, per the ticket's own
  ranking ("a column that doesn't add up ... reads as a bug"). Pinned by a named
  test, commented at the rounding site, and written up in the issue file. The
  alternative — put the tenth on water — is a one-line change if the display
  work later prefers it. Either way one of the two rules gives.
- Placeholder yeast percentages are not arbitrary. Each is what the ADR-0002
  ferment model (6 / activity) produces for that Preset's own schedule, so the
  numbers will not jump when Issue 04 replaces the literals with the model.
- Oil and sugar rows are gated on the *Preset's* value, not the live Recipe's,
  so zeroing the oil on a New York in Issue 03 does not make the control vanish.
- Leavening in the solver's denominator is the yeast type the user actually
  weighs out (dry = fresh / 3), not the stored fresh-yeast figure — otherwise a
  dry-yeast dough would be short on flour.
- solve() does not consult recipe.lock yet; commented as such. Ticket 02
  explicitly defers the ferment model, so the lock is inert machinery for now.
- Batch is adjusted with steppers, not typed entry. The shared slider with
  tappable numeric entry is Issue 03's component and every adjustable value
  should use it rather than growing a one-off here first.

Iteration 3 — 2026-08-16 — Issue 03: Adjust the Recipe freely  [DONE]
---------------------------------------------------------------------

What I did
- Built src/lib/components/AdjustableValue.svelte, the one control every
  adjustable number in the app will use: a range slider plus a readout that
  becomes a decimal-keypad field when tapped. Committed to its interface first,
  since Issues 04–06 bind proof hours and temperatures to it.
- Wired Hydration, salt, oil and sugar through it in Workbench.svelte, and added
  a fresh/dry yeast segmented control.
- Added two solver tests covering the yeast type swap. The Hydration test the
  ticket asks for already existed from Iteration 2.
- 17 tests. check, lint and build all clean; dev server smoke-tested.

Key decisions and reasoning
- THE YEAST TOGGLE CANNOT "CHANGE NOTHING ELSE". Second acceptance criteria
  collision in a row, same root as Iteration 2's. Fresh yeast weighs 3× dry, the
  Total Dough Weight is the Anchor, so swapping types moves ~3 g of mass that
  flour has to give up; the ingredients measured off flour shed a tenth with it.
  Under half a percent, and unavoidable short of breaking the column's sum. The
  test asserts what genuinely holds — percentages, schedule, total identical,
  yeast ratio 3 — then pins the drift below 0.5% of the batch so it fails if it
  ever grows. Written up in full in the issue file.
- The control is unit-agnostic: it moves a number between min and max and knows
  nothing about Baker's Percentages. The Workbench binds through a getter/setter
  pair (Svelte 5 function bindings) to convert fraction to percent. Cost is one
  conversion at four call sites; benefit is that hours and °C bind directly with
  no conversion at all in the next tickets.
- Ranges are wide (Hydration 40–110%, oil 0–30%) so the ends are somewhere no
  one arrives at by accident, not somewhere the app stops them. Typed values
  clamp into range rather than being rejected — the alternative is the slider
  and the number disagreeing.
- Step is per-control: 0.5 for Hydration, 0.1 for the small ingredients. A
  uniform 0.5 would have made Neapolitan's 2.8% salt unreachable by dragging.
- inputmode="decimal" on a text input, not type="number": it raises the right
  keypad without spinner arrows, and the draft can stay a string mid-edit.
  Commas are read as decimal points — a German keyboard offers a comma.
- Extracted presetUses() after the standards review flagged the "does this Style
  use oil/sugar" rule written twice, once per-field in the weights table and
  once generically for the controls.

Iteration 4 — 2026-08-16 — Issue 04: Proof Schedule and the ferment model  [DONE]
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------

What I did
- Added src/lib/dough/ferment.ts: the Q10 rate law from ADR-0002. Activity is
  Σ hours × 3^((celsius − 20) / 10) across the Stages; the Leavening a schedule
  asks for is the target divided by that. Not re-exported from the barrel.
- Deleted Style.freshYeastPercent and the five literals in styles.ts. The Preset
  no longer carries a Leavening figure — it is what its own schedule asks for.
  recipeFromStyle seeds Recipe.freshYeastPercent from the model instead.
- solve() now consults recipe.lock: with the schedule locked (always, for now)
  the Leavening comes from the model. The lock === "leavening" branch takes the
  stored figure as-is; the Elastic Stage is Issue 05.
- Workbench: a Proof section with the schedule as an ordered list of the Stages
  the dough actually passes through, ending at the Style's bake string, over
  five AdjustableValue controls — three durations, room temp, fridge temp. The
  standalone Bake section is gone; the bake line now closes the list.
- Added formatHours to src/lib/format.ts.
- 26 tests (was 17). All of check, lint, build and test:unit clean.

Key decisions and reasoning
- K IS IN PERCENTAGE POINTS. ADR-0002 writes "leavening% × activity = 6" with
  Leavening counted in percentage points; the code holds every Baker's
  Percentage as a fraction. The constant is therefore 6/100, and the first run
  without it put 184 g of yeast in a 1000 g dough. Commented at the constant.
  Confirmed correct by the five Presets solving to exactly the placeholder
  figures Iteration 2 wrote down (Neapolitan 0.737%, Same-day 0.963%, …), so
  nothing moved when the model replaced the literals.
- ONE ROOM TEMPERATURE, NOT TWO. The Bulk and the Warm-up share a worktop, so
  one control writes both. Two controls would let the user state a contradiction
  and answer a question they never asked. The Cold Proof keeps its own.
- ALL FIVE CONTROLS ALWAYS SHOWN. First cut hid the Warm-up and the fridge
  temperature when there was no Cold Proof. The spec review was right that this
  breaks "durations are all adjustable" — on Same-day, two of the five were
  unreachable, and the user had to invent a Cold Proof to get a Warm-up back. An
  absent Stage is one the user can still reach for. The list still omits it.
- Zero Activity reports zero Leavening rather than an infinity that would empty
  the Anchor's column. Every duration can be dragged to zero, so this is
  reachable, not theoretical.
- THE TESTS DELIBERATELY DO NOT KNOW THE CONSTANTS. I first wrote a test pinning
  Neapolitan to 0.737% and deleted it: the PRD forbids exactly that ("a test that
  has to be rewritten when a constant is retuned is testing the wrong thing").
  The Preset coverage asserts a 0.2%–2% fresh-yeast band, compared in fresh units
  so the four dry Styles are commensurable; the rest assert directions.
- Stage list variables are `stages`, not `steps` — CONTEXT.md lists "step" as a
  synonym to avoid, and the standards review caught it in both the code and my
  own comment. The Bulk control is labelled "Bulk" for the same reason.
